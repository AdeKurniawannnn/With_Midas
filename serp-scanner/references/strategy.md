# Strategic Framework and Best Practices

## Strategic Integration

This skill implements One Cloud Hub Indonesia's proven lead generation strategy:

- **Target Persona Focus**: C-Suite, technology leadership, technical management
- **Geographic Priority**: Jakarta, Surabaya, Bandung, Bali, Medan, and broader Indonesian regions
- **Industry Verticals**: Technology, fintech, manufacturing, e-commerce, healthcare, banking
- **Technology Readiness**: Cloud adoption, AI/GenAI interest indicators, digital transformation
- **Quality Scoring**: Lead tier framework (Tier 1-4) for interpreting targeting precision and lead potential

## Quality Indicators

This framework provides guidance for evaluating lead quality based on targeting precision:

- **Tier 1 (80-100)**: High-priority C-suite with specific industry and technology indicators
- **Tier 2 (60-79)**: Mid-level management with clear technology expertise
- **Tier 3 (40-59)**: Lower priority but relevant technical professionals
- **Tier 4 (<40)**: General prospects requiring additional qualification

## Best Practices

1. **Specific Targeting**: Combine location + seniority + industry for best results
2. **Use Quotes**: Multi-word terms need exact match: `"cloud computing"` `"financial technology"`
3. **Boolean Logic**: Use OR for synonyms, AND for required combinations
4. **Exclusions**: Systematically remove non-decision-makers with -recruiter -hr
5. **Quality over Quantity**: Focused targeting generates higher quality leads than broad searches

## Exclusion Strategy

Always apply this exclusion filter to remove non-decision-makers:
```
-recruiter -hr -human -resources -intern -student -graduate -trainer -consultant -freelance
```
